I'm a Master's student in Data Science (MSE) at Johns Hopkins University, in my final year of the program. I work in [Jennifer Hu's lab](https://glintlab.org), where I'm broadly interested in the intersection of cognition, language, and AI systems — how humans process and produce language, and how those insights can inform (and be informed by) machine learning models.

My interests span a few connected threads: **brain and cognition**, **language and AI systems**, **health and clinical tools**, and **behavior and decision-making**. I like working on problems where computational methods can shed light on how people actually think and communicate.

## Research

**Uniform Information Density in American Sign Language.** I'm building a computational linguistics pipeline investigating whether ASL production follows Uniform Information Density (UID) — the idea that speakers/signers distribute information evenly over time to ease processing. Using the ASLLRP corpus, I'm currently working on an n-gram language model (Kneser-Ney trigram) as a first step toward measuring information density across signed utterances.

**Baltimore Area Survey — AI Pre-registration.** With Kristina, I've been working on a project that uses GPT-4.1 to simulate survey responses ahead of the Baltimore Area Survey, comparing zero-shot and fine-tuned model predictions across modules like neighborhood perception, connectivity, housing, and transportation. The models are strong at predicting transportation and city/county-level patterns, but notably fail to capture political ideology — a finding I've been visualizing and preparing to present at a joint DSAI lab meeting.

## Coursework

This year I'm taking Optimization in Finance, Foundational Methods in Applied Math, Computational Genomics, and Cognitive AI (with Tianmin Shu) — a mix that reflects my interest in both the technical foundations of ML and its applications to cognition.

## Other Projects

I also enjoy exploring product ideas at the intersection of AI and public good. Recently I worked on a hackathon concept using Lovable and Granola for AI-assisted resource navigation in community clinical settings, and I've been scoping a drug safety monitoring concept for India built on public pharmacovigilance data (CDSCO/PvPI).
